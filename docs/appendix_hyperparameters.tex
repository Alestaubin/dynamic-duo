\section{Hyperparameters of the base algorithms}\label{app:hparams}

All five algorithms (Tent, ETA, EATA, SAR, COCA) adapt the affine parameters of the normalization layers with SGD (momentum $0.9$) at batch size $64$.
We tuned only the learning rate, separately for each model, on the four held-out corruptions (speckle noise, Gaussian blur, spatter, saturate; severity~5,
all $50{,}000$ images of each, models reset before each stream, one seed) and never on a test corruption.
The grids were $\{5{\cdot}10^{-5}, 10^{-4}, 2.5{\cdot}10^{-4}, 5{\cdot}10^{-4}, 10^{-3}\}$ for ResNet-50 and $\{10^{-4}, 2.5{\cdot}10^{-4}, 5{\cdot}10^{-4}, 10^{-3}, 2.5{\cdot}10^{-3}\}$ for ViT-B/16.
The two models adapt independently, so run~$i$ uses the $i$-th value of both grids, and each model keeps the value with the best standalone mean accuracy over the held-out streams.
Where the best value was the end of a grid we added one point: $5{\cdot}10^{-5}$ for the ViT under Tent, and $5{\cdot}10^{-3}$ (ViT) and $2{\cdot}10^{-3}$ (ResNet-50) under SAR; for SAR's ViT the gain from the last step was only $0.5$ points, so its optimum may lie slightly beyond.
COCA couples the two models, so it was tuned by pairs of learning rates, and also run at its published pair.
Everything else is the official setting and was not tuned: entropy margin $0.4\ln K$, redundancy threshold $0.05$ (ETA, EATA), Fisher weight $2000$ estimated on $2000$ clean images (EATA; taken from a held-out slice of the ImageNet training set, not from the validation images ImageNet-C is rendered from), SAM radius $0.05$, no model recovery, and, as in its code, no adaptation of the normalization parameters of the last ResNet stage and of the last three ViT blocks (SAR, as a base algorithm), and $10$ temperature steps per batch (COCA, as in its code).
Table~\ref{tab:hparams} gives the values and the learning rates of the official code for each architecture.
For ResNet-50 they agree for Tent and ETA and differ by a factor $2$ to $4$ for EATA, SAR and COCA; on the held-out streams the official value is within $0.3$ points of ours for EATA and COCA and about $2$ points lower for SAR.
For ViT-B/16 ours are lower than the official $10^{-3}$ for Tent ($20\times$; at $10^{-3}$ Tent falls to $44.5\%$ on the held-out streams, below the unadapted $48.8\%$), ETA and EATA ($2\times$), higher for SAR ($5\times$; $10^{-3}$ scores $57.9\%$ against $59.9\%$), and equal for COCA.
Our ViT-B/16 is torchvision's ImageNet-1k model rather than the AugReg model of those papers, so its optimum need not carry over, while our ResNet-50 uses the original weights the papers use.
